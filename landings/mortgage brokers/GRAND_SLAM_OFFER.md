### **3. PROBLEMS vs. SOLUTIONS DEEP-DIVE (Surgically Aligned with MVP)**

#### **Problem 1: The Missed Call Leak**
*   **The Pain:** Brokers miss 20% to 30% of incoming calls. If a prospect hits a voicemail, 85% leave no message, and 67% call a competitor within 30 minutes. 
*   **The Solution:** **24/7 Intake Concierge.** Conversational voice assistant that screens inbound prospects and books them directly into your calendar.

#### **Problem 2: The 2026 Refinance Cliff (Lazy Bank Renewal)**
*   **The Pain:** 1.8 Million fixed-rate mortgages expire in 2026. Lenders target these clients directly with direct, "one-click" auto-renewal offers. If your clients call you out of hours to check: "Is this bank rate good?" and hit your voicemail, they give up and auto-renew with the lender. You lose the client forever.
*   **The Solution:** **Refinance Inbound Protection.** Intercepts these urgent out-of-hours inbound inquiries, securing them in your calendar before they take the lazy lender option.

#### **Problem 3: The 45-Hour Week & Mental Burnout**
*   **The Pain:** 62% of mortgage professionals work over 45 hours a week, and 21% report high anxiety. Constantly staying on-call to avoid losing leads ruins mental health and leads to industry exit.
*   **The Solution:** **Nights & Weekends Switch.** Turn off your phone at 6:00 PM without anxiety. Decana answers, qualifies, and books your appointments while you recharge.

#### **Problem 4: Status Call Distraction (Zero Revenue Calls)**
*   **The Pain:** Anxious clients, solicitors, and surveyors calling to ask "where is my application?" do not make you money but break focus, eating up hours of high-value advice time.
*   **The Solution:** **Status-Call Deflector.** Decana identifies non-revenue status requests and routes them into a structured queue, shielding your active working sessions.

#### **Problem 5: MCOB Audit Readiness**
*   **The Pain:** MCOB rules require firms to supply complete call records to regulators within 2 days of a request. Compiling these logs manually under audit pressure is incredibly stressful.
*   **The Solution:** **MCOB 2-Day Audit Logs.** Decana transcribes and organizes every call securely, allowing you to extract and deliver complete records to auditors in under two minutes.
