# Landing Page Structure: Decana (Verified & Compliant)
## Positioning: The "Broker's Safety Net" for UK Mortgage Professionals

This landing page is designed using **Alex Hormozi's Grand Slam Offer** and **Russell Brunson's High-Converting Landing Page Flow**, modified for high-compliance financial services.

### Key Copywriting adjustments (Compliant & Honest)
*   **No Absolute Claims:** No "100%", "FCA-compliant" (FCA doesn't certify software), or "UK's first" (other platforms like My Mortgage Mate exist). Instead, use "supports your obligations" and "sovereign UK security".
*   **Aligned Metrics:** Average commission of **£1,000** used consistently throughout.
*   **Realistic Funnel Math:** The ROI calculator factors in a realistic **30% conversion rate** from booked consultation to completed case (rather than treating bookings as guaranteed money).
*   **Correct Refi Cliff Mechanics:** Focuses on intercepting the client's urgent "Should I renew?" inbound inquiry before they auto-accept direct bank renewals, rather than assuming they dial out.
*   **Burnout as Emotional Lever:** Focuses on the "62% work >45 hrs" and "21% anxiety" metrics to appeal to sole practitioners.
